How Sizing Works

Every kit is priced and packed for your group size

The same three tiers apply across the whole catalog. Pick a kit, tell us your headcount, and we handle the rest.

📦

One tier system. Every kit in the catalog. One quote.

Every tier includes materials sized to the group, a simple instruction card, and activity guidance, so there's no sharing and no running short mid-event.

Pricing is materials plus kitting labor. The bigger your group gets, the better your per-kid rate gets too, which most kit vendors have backwards.

TierGroup sizeBuilt for
Troop Up to 25 kids Materials sized 1:1 per child, no sharing required.
Event 26–75 kids Bulk-packed by station rather than by child, which is where the per-kid rate drops.
Outreach 76–150+ kids Station kits plus reusable tools, minimal per-kid consumables.
