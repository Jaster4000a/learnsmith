A preview of what facilitators get

Every kit ships with a full lesson plan, not just materials. Grade-band instructions, safety notes, and a facilitator script a volunteer with no teaching background can run. Here's a preview.

Grade Band

Grades 3–5

Learning Objective

Design and build a protective structure to prevent an egg from breaking during a drop test.

The Science Explained

When the egg hits the ground, it experiences a sudden impact force. Using soft materials spreads this force out over more time and surface area, which prevents the shell from cracking.
