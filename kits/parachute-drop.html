Example Lesson Plan

A preview of what facilitators get

Every kit comes with a full lesson plan alongside the materials: grade-band instructions, safety notes, and a facilitator script simple enough for a volunteer with no teaching background to run. This one's built for Grades 3–5, and the goal is straightforward: construct a parachute that slows the descent of a falling object.

The science comes down to drag. As the canopy falls, it catches air, and that air resistance pushes back against gravity, slowing the egg's descent instead of just cushioning the impact on landing.

This is a preview. The full lesson plan (facilitator script, safety notes, and standards alignment) ships with every kit.
